Frequently Answered Commercial Terms

Clear, transparent answers for international procurement managers.

What Incoterms do you support?

We operate standard Incoterms 2020: FOB (Free on Board from Indian ports), CFR (Cost and Freight), and CIF (Cost, Insurance and Freight) directly to your designated global port.

How is quality verified before dispatch?

Every batch is tested in NABL-accredited laboratories for moisture, pesticide MRLs, microbiological purity, and physical grading. We can also accommodate third-party inspection (SGS, Eurofins) prior to container stuffing.

Can prospective buyers request physical samples?

Yes. We dispatch certified production-representative samples via express international air couriers (DHL / FedEx) accompanied by full phytosanitary clearance and preliminary lab certificates.

What are your standard commercial payment terms?

We work with Irrevocable Letters of Credit (LC at sight from prime international banks), Telegraphic Transfer (TT) with deposit on proforma confirmation, and customized structured terms for contracted recurring buyers.
