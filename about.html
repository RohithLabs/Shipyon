About Us

ABOUT US

Shipyon stands as South India’s premier Export Import and Global Trade Partner, offering direct farm-gate procurement and certified trade supply chains across 50+ countries.

Headquartered in Dharapuram, Tamil Nadu — at the geographic crossroads of India's richest agricultural belts — Shipyon was established to transform fragmented farm gate trade into predictable, certified commodity flows. By eliminating speculative intermediaries and providing verified grade specifications, refrigerated cold chains, and vessel-level traceability, we ensure every shipment from Cochin, Tuticorin, and Chennai reaches international buyers in prime condition.

Our cutting-edge sourcing network eliminates speculative intermediaries, ensuring strict phytosanitary compliance, temperature-controlled cold chains, and complete vessel transparency. From fresh produce and plantation spices to grains and industrial textiles, Shipyon provides the governance to enhance your global supply chain with predictability and unmatched quality.
