Our Story

Rooted in South India. Trusted Globally.

Established in Dharapuram, Tamil Nadu — at the geographical crossroads of India's richest agricultural heartland — Shipyon was founded to transform fragmented farm gate produce into predictable, world-class export commodity flows.

We operate through direct long-term partnerships with over 500 verified agricultural growers, spice plantations, and industrial manufacturers. By eliminating intermediaries and instituting precision grading, refrigerated cold chains, and end-to-end documentation, we ensure every shipment from Cochin or Tuticorin reaches international buyers in peak condition.
