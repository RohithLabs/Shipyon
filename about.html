Rooted in Dharapuram. Connected to Global Ports.

Shipyon was established in Dharapuram, Tamil Nadu — situated in the fertile Amaravathi river basin, renowned as one of South India's oldest and most respected agricultural trading corridors.

Surrounded by premier commodity clusters — the world-famous turmeric markets of Erode, the cardamom auctions of Theni, the coconut groves of Pollachi, and the textile spinning mills of Tiruppur — Shipyon was founded to solve a fundamental deficiency in international merchant trade: the opacity and volatility created by multiple speculative intermediaries.

By placing our operational center directly at the source, we establish contractual relationships with verified farmers, growers' cooperatives, and processing units. We provide global buyers with unadulterated provenance, certified lab analysis, and guaranteed container allocations.

Our Mission & Guiding Principles

Our Mission

To deliver premium South Indian agricultural commodities and manufactured goods to international buyers through disciplined farm-gate procurement, strict multi-point laboratory analysis, and synchronized logistics execution.

🔭

Our Vision

To become South India's premier high-trust export house, recognized worldwide by commercial buyers, food conglomerates, and industrial distributors for uncompromising quality integrity, fair farm pricing, and shipment reliability.

Radical Transparency

Every shipment is backed by verified laboratory certificates, moisture profiles, pesticide MRL analysis, and origin geo-tagging. We never conceal product provenance or grade substitutions.

Sustainable global trade begins at the soil. We maintain direct, long-term procurement agreements with South Indian farmer collectives, ensuring prompt payments and fair-value pricing.
