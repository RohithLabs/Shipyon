Five Integrated Trade Solutions

Every stage managed with institutional precision to eliminate international buyer risk.

// SOLUTION_01

01. Direct Origin Sourcing & Procurement

Direct aggregation at farm-gate collection centers and primary producer cooperatives across South India. Batch segregation, farm traceability, and transparent forward contracting without intermediary speculation.

// SOLUTION_02

02. Laboratory Quality & Inspection

Rigorous laboratory analysis across NABL-accredited test facilities. Pesticide MRL screens, aflatoxin verification, physical grading, and moisture profiling conforming to European Commission, US FDA, and GCC standards.

// SOLUTION_03

03. Export Documentation & Compliance

Comprehensive international trade compliance: Phytosanitary Certificates, Fumigation Certificates, Certificates of Origin (COO), Commercial Invoices, Bill of Lading, and pre-shipment SGS or Eurofins verification.

// SOLUTION_04

04. Global Freight & Cold Chain Logistics

Specialized Reefer (refrigerated) container bookings, active atmosphere management, real-time satellite temperature loggers, and express multi-modal dispatch via Tuticorin, Chennai, Cochin, and Mumbai ports.

// SOLUTION_05

05. Private Labeling & Custom Packaging

End-to-end white-label packaging solutions: Nitrogen-flushed consumer pouches, printed master cartons, food-grade vacuum packs, and heavy-duty FIBC jumbo bags labeled to destination country barcode specifications.

// 02_QUALITY_PROTOCOL

5-Stage Quality & Compliance Protocol

Zero compromise. From initial farm-gate aggregation to final seal inspection at port.

[STAGE_01]

Farm-gate Sourcing & Batch Intake

Direct collection from audited growers with lot segregation and field traceability logging.

[STAGE_02]

NABL Laboratory Verification

Testing for moisture levels, pesticide MRLs, aflatoxins, and microbiological safety.

[STAGE_03]

Precision Processing & Sorting

Sortex optical cleaning, color sorting, and vacuum packaging in temperature-stable facilities.

[STAGE_04]

Phytosanitary Certification

Official inspection by Indian Plant Quarantine authority with fumigation documentation.

[STAGE_05]

Container Sealing & Pre-Dispatch

Final digital container inspection, data logger placement, customs clearance, and vessel dispatch.
